# Privacy Policy for Search Portal Extension

**Last Updated**: January 31, 2026

## Overview

Search Portal is committed to protecting your privacy. This extension operates entirely on your local device and does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

## Data Collection

### What We DON'T Collect
- ❌ No personal information
- ❌ No browsing history
- ❌ No search queries
- ❌ No analytics or tracking
- ❌ No cookies
- ❌ No user accounts or authentication

### What We DO Store (Locally Only)
- ✅ Your pinned apps/bookmarks (stored in browser's LocalStorage)
- ✅ Your to-do list items (stored in browser's LocalStorage)
- ✅ Your notes content (stored in browser's LocalStorage)
- ✅ Your preferences (search engine, weather city, etc.)
- ✅ Cached app icons (for offline use)

**Important**: All data is stored locally on your device using the browser's LocalStorage API. This data never leaves your computer.

## Permissions

### Storage Permission
- **Why**: To save your preferences, bookmarks, notes, and to-do items locally
- **Scope**: Local device only
- **Access**: Only this extension can access this data

## Third-Party Services

### Optional External Requests
The extension may make requests to the following services ONLY when you actively use certain features:

1. **App Icons**:
   - Clearbit Logo API (`logo.clearbit.com`)
   - Google Favicon API (`google.com/s2/favicons`)
   - Used to fetch icons for your pinned apps
   - Can be avoided by providing custom icon URLs

2. **Google Fonts**:
   - Used for typography
   - Cached for offline use after first load

3. **Ambient Sounds**:
   - Google Actions sounds (`actions.google.com`)
   - Freesound CDN (`cdn.freesound.org`)
   - Only loaded when you activate ambient sounds

4. **Weather** (if implemented):
   - Weather data fetched when you enter a city name
   - No automatic location tracking

## Data Security

- All user data is stored using browser's built-in LocalStorage
- No data transmission to external servers
- No cloud backup or sync
- Data persists only on your local device

## Children's Privacy

This extension does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age.

## Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the extension's update notes.

## Data Deletion

To delete all your data:
1. Right-click the extension icon
2. Select "Remove from Chrome"
3. All local data will be automatically deleted

Alternatively, you can clear individual items:
- Bookmarks: Use the delete button in the Manage Apps widget
- To-dos: Use the delete button next to each task
- Notes: Clear the notes widget manually

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the Chrome Web Store support page.

## Consent

By using this extension, you consent to this privacy policy.
